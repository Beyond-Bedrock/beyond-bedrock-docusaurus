# Banner Markers Add-on

![Banner Markers Thumbnail](/img/addons/banner-markers/thumbnail.webp)

Have you ever wanted to mark your bases, waypoints, farms, and points of interest directly on your in-game maps like in Java Edition?

**Banner Markers** brings Java Edition style banner waypoints and floating in-world names to Minecraft Bedrock Edition!

<SectionSpacer 
  title="Key Features"
  type="plain"
  thickness={8}
  font="Minecraft Ten"
  color="linear-gradient(90deg, #f39c12, #e74c3c)"
  textColor="linear-gradient(90deg, #f39c12, #e74c3c)"
/>

- **Java-Style Banner Markers:** Place colored marker icons matching banner colors directly onto filled maps.
- **Custom Name Display on Maps:** Banners renamed in an anvil have their custom name rendered right beneath the marker icon on the map.
- **In-World 3D Floating Names:** Named banners display native 3D in-world text labels floating above them.
- **All 16 Colors Supported:** Full support for every standard Minecraft banner color (White, Orange, Magenta, Light Blue, Yellow, Lime, Pink, Gray, Light Gray, Cyan, Purple, Blue, Brown, Green, Red, Black).
- **100% Entity-Free & Lag-Free:** Uses modern Script API text primitives and world coordinate tracking—no invisible dummy entities, hitboxes, or performance drops.
- **Automatic Cleanup:** Breaking or removing a banner instantly cleans up its map icon and 3D floating text.

<SectionSpacer 
  title="How to Use"
  type="plain"
  thickness={8}
  font="Minecraft Ten"
  color="linear-gradient(90deg, #f39c12, #e74c3c)"
  textColor="linear-gradient(90deg, #f39c12, #e74c3c)"
/>

1. **Equip Map in Offhand:** Place a **Filled Map** into your offhand slot.
2. **Hold Banner in Main Hand:** Hold any color **Banner** in your main hand. You can rename the banner first using an **Anvil** if you want custom text.
3. **Place the Banner:** Place the banner onto the ground or against a wall in the Overworld.
4. **View on Map & in World:** Your map will immediately show the colored banner marker with your custom text underneath, and a clean 3D text label will appear above the banner in the world.
5. **Remove Markers:** Simply break the banner to remove the marker from your map and clear the in-world text.
